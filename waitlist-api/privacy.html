Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 23, 2026

pickupsports.us ("we," "us"), a sole proprietorship, operates the Pick Up waitlist signup so you can be notified when the Pick Up app opens for signups. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist, we collect the email address and phone number you provide.

How we use it

We use your email and phone number solely to:

  • Send you a one-time confirmation email and text when you join the waitlist.
  • Notify you when Pick Up opens for signups.

We do not sell your information to third parties, and we do not use it for advertising targeting.

Who we share it with

We use the following processors solely to deliver the messages above:

  • Resend — to send confirmation and notification emails.
  • Twilio — to send confirmation and notification text messages.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply to SMS messages. Reply STOP to any text to opt out of future messages. Reply HELP for assistance.

Your choices

You can unsubscribe from emails at any time using the link in any email we send, and from texts by replying STOP. To request deletion of your information, contact us at mitch@pickupsports.us.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email mitch@pickupsports.us.
