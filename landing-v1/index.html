Why we made it

We got tired of leagues.

A few things kept happening to us, so we built the app we wished existed.

We hated committing to leagues we couldn't even show up to.

We could never find games that matched our skill level.

We hated hunting for leagues and paying fees just to play.

Most of all, we wanted to bring back the vibe of the sandlot: play when you want, on your schedule.
